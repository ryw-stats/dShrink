Codes for infant death data, Post-ERCP data, and simulation are in separate folders

Codes are named after the figure/table/results they produce. p is the dimension of the target parameter beta_T, q is the dimension of the source parameter alpha_S, n and m are the target and source sample sizes. Adjustments in these parameters may be required to produce the results of different panels of a figure.

Functions not named after what they produce are auxiliary functions required for other functions

"Res_xxx.R" indicates the function is used to produce results to plot a Figure
"Plot_xxx.R" indicates the function is used to plot a Figure based on results generated by some other "Res_xxx.R" functions
